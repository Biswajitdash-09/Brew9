## BREW 9 — Global Brand Style

Use this style concept in every prompt:

* Brand: **BREW 9**
* Tagline: **YOUR 9-TO-9 BREW**
* Style: minimal, premium roadside coffee startup
* Colors: Coffee Brown `#3A2418`, Cream `#F5EBDD`, Warm Beige `#D8C2A8`, Black `#151515`
* Typography: **Manrope**
* Design language: modern Indian roadside café + premium minimalist branding
* Avoid: generic Starbucks-like branding, excessive gradients, neon colors, clutter, cartoon style
* Photography: warm natural light, realistic, premium editorial photography

### Important for Antigravity

For the **actual website**, I would **not use AI-generated images for the logo** if Antigravity can create SVG. Tell it:

> **“Create the BREW 9 logo as a clean editable SVG using the approved brand mark, not as a raster image.”**

For product/hero/lifestyle photography, use generated **WebP/AVIF** assets and keep them optimized.

The most important thing is **consistency**: once you generate the primary BREW 9 logo, use that exact logo/reference for the kiosk, cups, packaging, social assets, and website—not a newly interpreted “BREW 9” logo each time.
